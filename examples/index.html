Florametric examples

  • Static plants in cards: Florametric.render() and preset="static", transparent backgrounds, light and dark pages.
  • Feature toggles: a live garden with a switch for every trait, and the matching garden.set() call.
  • Web components: <florametric-plant>, <florametric-garden> and <florametric-meadow> configured with attributes.
  • node examples/node-text.js rose: plain-text ASCII in a terminal.
